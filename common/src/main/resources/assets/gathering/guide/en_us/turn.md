# Playing a turn

## The keys that run a turn

- {key:untap_all} untaps everything you have.
- {key:draw} draws a card.
- {key:pass_turn} or Enter ends your turn. Nothing untaps and nothing is drawn for the next player.

When the turn comes round to you, you hear it and the top row says so.

## Tapping, life and counters

- Point at a card and press {key:tap} to tap it, {key:untap} to untap it.
- Plus and minus put a +1/+1 counter on the card you point at, or change your life when you point at nothing.
- A card's menu has every kind of counter under **Counters**.

## Finding an action

- {key:palette} opens a box: type what you want to do and choose it. Every action is in there, including the ones with no key.
- {key:show_log} shows the log, which says who did what.
- A misclick can be taken back with **Undo** on the table's menu.
