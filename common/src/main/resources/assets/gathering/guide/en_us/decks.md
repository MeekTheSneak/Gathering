# Decks

## Getting a deck

- Type **/gathering import** with nothing after it. Paste a decklist, or a link to an Archidekt deck, and press **Import**.
- Or pick up a card and right-click it onto another: the two become a deck.
- Sitting down at a table empty-handed, you may be offered a deck to borrow. A borrowed deck is for playing with and cannot be kept.

## Changing a deck

Right-click a deck to open its list.

- Rest the pointer on a row to read that card.
- Left-click a row to take a copy out.
- Right-click a row to move it to the sideboard or the command zone.
- Hold a card and right-click the deck to put it in.
