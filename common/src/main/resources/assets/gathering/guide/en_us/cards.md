# Moving cards

## Playing and moving

- Drag a card from your hand onto the felt to play it.
- Drag a card on the felt to move it, or onto a zone to put it there: your library, graveyard or exile.
- Drag from a zone to take its top card.
- Hold a press on a zone or a stack for a moment to lift the whole pile, then drop it somewhere else.

A pile you just put cards into glows in your seat's color for a moment, so everybody sees where they went.

## Menus and keys

- Right-click a card for everything it can do, and right-click the felt for everything else.
- {key:to_exile}, {key:to_graveyard} and {key:to_library_bottom_random} send the card you point at to exile, to the graveyard, or to the bottom of your library in no order.
- {key:turn_over} turns a card face up or face down.
- Shift-click cards, or drag a box around them, to select several. G stacks the selection.

## Reading

Point at any card and hold {key:read} to read it in full. Nobody can tell you did.

Right-click a card and choose **Write on it** to leave a note everybody at the table can read.

## What stays hidden

Your hand and the order of your library are yours alone, even to somebody watching. To show your hand, right-click the felt and choose **Show my hand to**.
